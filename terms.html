Finance Terms of Use

Finance is personal software for its owner’s private, non-commercial use. It is not a public financial service.

Account information

The application may display information obtained from connected financial institutions through Enable Banking. Displayed balances and transactions can be delayed, incomplete or temporarily unavailable and should not be treated as formal bank statements.

No financial advice

Budgets, forecasts, categories and automation suggestions are informational tools and do not constitute financial, legal, tax or investment advice.

Payments

The application does not initiate real payments. Transfer previews and automation rules are simulations unless explicitly stated otherwise. Real payments must be authorised and completed through an appropriate regulated provider or banking application.

Security

The user is responsible for protecting the Windows account, Finance password, local private keys and computer on which the application runs.

Availability

The software is provided for personal use without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or error-free financial data. Important balances and payment instructions should be verified with the relevant bank.

Consent

Connecting an account confirms that the user owns or is authorised to access that account and consents to retrieving its information for display in Finance.
