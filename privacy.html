Finance Privacy Notice

Finance is a private, single-user desktop application operated locally by its user. It is not offered as a public or commercial service.

Data processed

With your explicit consent, Finance retrieves account names, balances and transaction information from accounts you connect through Enable Banking.

Storage

Imported financial information is stored locally on your Windows computer in the application’s SQLite database. Finance does not operate a cloud database, analytics service or advertising system.

Credentials

Finance does not request or store bank usernames or passwords. Bank authentication is performed by your bank and Enable Banking. The Enable Banking application private key remains on your computer and is never exposed to the user interface.

Purpose and retention

Data is used only to provide personal financial tracking, budgeting and transaction analysis. It remains on the computer until the user removes the application data.

Sharing

Finance does not sell or share financial data. Enable Banking and the connected bank process information as required to provide the account-information connection under their respective notices.

Your control

You can revoke bank consent through your bank or Enable Banking and remove local application data at any time.
